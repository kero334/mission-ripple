# Nuri character and dialogue

Nuri is a small, friendly floating robot with white shell, dark face, cyan eyes and warm side accents. In the active 2D game she follows smoothly without blocking movement, has a floating shadow and visual idle/happy/thinking/warning/speak moods. Relevant image assets are under `project/public/assets/characters/` and `project/public/assets/ui/`; keys and expression switches are in `src/scene.ts`. The portrait accompanies DOM dialogue.

Her role is scientist-guide: welcome the player, point to facilities, explain that a rover reading is real, ask children to check the surrounding sequence, name unknowns, frame the fictional scheduling choice, and explain immediate/delayed consequences. `src/science/ui.ts` sends `ripple-dialogue` for mission prompts; `src/scene.ts` sends it for entry, equipment, Nuri contact and exploration. `src/main.ts` renders localized text. English and Arabic text live in these modules and `world-data.ts`, not a separate translation file. L changes locale; locale is not persisted.

Current help consists of objective marker, E prompt, mission card route and short Nuri prompts. A configurable three-level assistance system, voluntary help escalation and persisted hint state are **not implemented**. The marker target follows mission stage, limiting stale guidance; general exploration prompts still rely on proximity. No active 2D voice playback or matching new mission narration files were found. The old 3D game's voice work is historical; do not claim those recordings match new dialogue or spend audio API credits without authorization.
