# NASA science integration

The authoritative research handoff lives outside this archive at the original Windows path in [PROJECT_HANDOFF.md](PROJECT_HANDOFF.md). Read its `START_HERE.md`, `09_GAME_CODEX_HANDOFF/GAME_CODEX_TASK_PROMPT.md`, `MISSION_SELECTION_FRAMEWORK.md`, `05_MISSION_EVIDENCE/mission_evidence_matrix.csv`, `06_SIMULATION_FOUNDATIONS/scientific_limitations.md`, `10_REPORTS/validation_report.md`, and `source_audit.md` before selecting new science. The research package reports 191 preserved originals, 300,550 heterogeneous inventoried records, 27 dataset products, 20 findings, seven cases, 22 opportunities, six blocked opportunities, 35 project tests and 6,846 integrity checks. These are **package-reported counts**, not independent scientific certification.

`SOURCE_REGISTRY.json` authorizes seven source families: NASA Open APIs, DONKI, OSDR, RadLab, Mars Trek, CSA STEM and CSA Food. Raw records, derived findings, education copy and game assumptions are separate levels. A finding is an analysis of an original observation, not a new instrument measurement. The game's `src/science/evidence-v1.json` is a compact immutable versioned subset of a NASA RadLab Curiosity RAD case. `tools/build_science_snapshot.py` can regenerate it from the external research package; `tools/science-qa.mjs` compares exact selected rows and source SHA. Those two workflows are **not standalone from this archive**. The browser game itself is standalone after dependencies install.

For original data, use NASA RadLab `MSL-RAD-Surface` CSV `01_ORIGINAL_DATA/RADLAB/phase2/MSL_RAD_surface_2024.csv`, SHA-256 `baea6261262f499f02259fbfba986973038c88a7b03f4f1b2136d6e346132a11`, URL `https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/data-api/`. The 20 May 2024 Curiosity RAD retrieved peak is **303.98798 µGy/hour at 06:15:19 UTC**. A derived episode counted 92 retrieved readings above an **exploratory descriptive** 30 µGy/hour cutoff over 14.4047 hours; previous-day and event-day medians are 3.8454966 and 42.504765 µGy/hour. The game shows rounded visual teaching values while exact records remain in a source drawer.

Critical limits: these are rover Mars-surface absorbed dose-rate readings, **not astronaut dose, Habitat dose, human safe/danger thresholds or proof of a solar-event cause**. No dose equivalent is present in the cited export. Sensor quality flags and local transfer are unknown. Do not infer human exposure, shelter performance, safe duration, health damage, plant yield, oxygen or water rates. DONKI coincidence does not establish causation; ISS measurements are not Martian-surface data; Mars Trek colored tiles are not numeric slopes. Fictional work slots and branch flags are labeled training-game values. NASA/CSA attribution does not imply endorsement.
