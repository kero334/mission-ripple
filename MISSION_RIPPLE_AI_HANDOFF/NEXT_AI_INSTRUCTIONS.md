1. Open the extracted `project/` directory, not the original 3D game. Read `../START_HERE.md`, `../CURRENT_STATE.md`, `../KNOWN_ISSUES.md`, `project/docs/SCIENCE_MISSION_ARCHITECTURE.md`, and `project/src/science/mission.ts`. Review `project/src/scene.ts`, `project/src/main.ts`, `project/src/science/ui.ts` before changing integration.
